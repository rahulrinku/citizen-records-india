India Stack Identity Architecture & Legal Compliance

          
Why public open APIs are structured with privacy safeguards

        

      


      

        
        

          

            
            UIDAI & Privacy Safeguards
          

          

            Direct 12-digit UID numbers and core biometrics cannot be exposed or queried on public web interfaces. Legitimate verification is strictly processed via Offline Paperless e-KYC (XML with 4-digit code), Masked Tokens, or regulated AUA/KUA gateways.
          

        


        

          

            
            DPDP Act 2023 Consent Rule
          

          

            Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, every verification query against national databases (PAN, Parivahan, GSTN) mandates unbundled, informed citizen consent. Queries generate immutable digital audit signatures.
          

        


        

          

            
            API Setu & DigiLocker Gateway
          

          

            The Government of India provides unified sandbox integrations through apisetu.gov.in and data.gov.in. Developers authenticate using Mutual TLS (mTLS) and asymmetric keys, allowing verification without saving unauthorized copies.
